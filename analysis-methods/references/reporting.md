# Turn analysis into a useful report

Start with the question answered, the strongest supported finding and its
practical implication. Match structure and depth to the audience and evidence.
Use the requested template and language. There is no minimum page, chart, word,
chapter or framework count, and no consulting-brand persona to imitate.

For a substantive decision report, include the elements that help its reader:

- **Answer and scope:** what was studied, the conclusion and its conditions.
- **Findings:** evidence, comparisons, calculations and what they imply.
- **Alternatives:** tradeoffs and why a different interpretation or option may win.
- **Uncertainty:** material assumptions, missing evidence and sensitivity.
- **Action:** the next decision, experiment or step warranted by the findings.
- **Methods and sources:** enough detail to understand and reproduce the analysis.

An informational overview need not recommend an action. Do not invent an owner,
deadline, target, budget, or estimate to fill a template. Suggest values only as
explicit proposals when they help the requested decision.

## Charts and tables

Build analytical charts from computed or sourced data. Give them legible labels,
units, time ranges and meaningful denominators. Mark forecasts and scenario
assumptions. Explain missing data and transformations; do not draw invented
values to complete a chart. Avoid image generation for quantitative chart data.

Use comparisons, trend charts, cohort tables, driver bridges or sensitivity
tables where they clarify the result. Check axes, visual scales, legends and
uncertainty against the actual numbers. A caption should explain the finding
without exceeding the evidence.

## Final analytical review

Check the final summary, chart titles and recommendations against the analysis,
not only the body text. Confirm period/currency/unit consistency, arithmetic,
source locations, comparable populations and the conditions under which a
recommendation changes. Preserve unresolved conflicts and material limitations.
Check that each method promised in the plan was executed or explicitly narrowed.

Analytical review and rendering review are separate. Apply the relevant output
skill to check the final file or hosted view. Formatting checks cannot establish
factual support; sound analysis cannot establish that a page renders correctly.
Reuse checked analysis across formats and keep working ledgers and scripts local
unless requested or necessary for the user to use the result.
