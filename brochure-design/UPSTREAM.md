## Included and modified

- Two layout families, three actual A4 HTML/CSS book variants: `old-money-book`
  (8 pages), `sport-ivory` (12) and `sport-night` (12). Technical IDs retain source mapping.
- Page CSS, only the colour math needed for rebranding/checks, a local-only
  initializer/loader/renderer, preflight and offline tests.

  or claimed client facts. Paper/grain SVGs and transparent cut-out slots are
  original replacements. Sport Night's monochrome noise is reproducible with
  `scripts/generate_newsprint.py`; hashes/provenance are in `ASSET-MANIFEST.json`.

  Big Shoulders Display 5.3.0 and Source Serif 4 5.3.1 (Fontsource versions).
  Keep full family licenses and copyrights. The 22 unmodified Latin/Latin-Extended
  WOFF2 files retain source tarball pins/integrity and SHA-256 in `assets/fonts/manifest.json`.
  CJK fonts are not bundled. Commercial aliases are removed.
- Complete required-copy matching, failed/missing PDF-font checks treated as errors,
  fresh project-confined output, local resource checks and website-JavaScript blocking.
  This is defense in depth for reviewed authored HTML, not a hostile-input sandbox.
