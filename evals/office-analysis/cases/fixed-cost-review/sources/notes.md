operating profit is gross profit minus operating expense. The planned cost of
sales comprises a committed 60000 quarterly fixed charge plus a variable charge
of 15% of recognized revenue. The fixed charge remained 60000 in actuals.
There are no unit-quantity, product-mix or supplier-rate records in this packet.

20000 was cancelled. Acceptance has not yet occurred. Delivery reports using
expedited outsourcing during the quarter but has not supplied its amount. The
records do not establish that outsourcing caused every cost variance. Operating
expense categories have not been reconciled. A 30000 receipt for next quarter's
