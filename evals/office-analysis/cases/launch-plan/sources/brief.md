# Fictional two-week launch

Budget ceiling: CNY 24000. Required one-time setup: CNY 6000. This setup fee stays
unchanged if the lead target changes. All remaining spend purchases channel
placements. The target is 120 qualified leads. A lead must have an identified
team need, a valid work email and permission for follow-up. Neither an increase
in budget nor a lower target has been approved.

Proposed channel allocation: search 6000, industry email 12000. A planning draft
estimates 80 leads and an all-in cost per lead of 300. It proposes a 36000 total
budget if the owner insists on 120 leads while retaining the same channel mix.
This is a draft to assess, not an approved requirement.

Channel history: one small test per channel. Search spent 3000 for 10 qualified
leads; industry email spent 4000 for 20. Quotes, scalable capacity and changes in
unit economics are unknown. Do not assume the history guarantees future results.

The marketer and writer each have at most 8 hours per week. The draft assigns
the marketer 3 hours of setup review, 3 buying placements, 2 coordinating partners
and 2 measurement hours in week 1. Week 2 assigns 3 placement-management hours,
3 lead-checking hours and 2 reporting hours. The writer has 7 hours of work in
each week. Setup review and initial tracking must precede paid launch; partner
coordination must precede placements. Measurement and lead qualification cannot
be dropped. Activity-hour estimates are the supplied planning basis. Do not
assume an extra person, unpaid overtime, lower prices or faster work. You may
propose moving work across weeks, splitting activities or reducing scope if you
identify the resulting launch and capacity consequences.
