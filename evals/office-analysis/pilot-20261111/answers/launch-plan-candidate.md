# Launch recommendation

Proceed with preparation, but hold paid commitments until the owner approves a feasible budget and timetable. **Propose retaining the 120-qualified-lead target and the channel mix at CNY 33,000**, conditional on quotes and capacity checks. This exceeds the ceiling by CNY 9,000 (37.5%); it is an estimate requiring approval, not an authorized budget or a guaranteed result. No source-supported plan currently meets both 120 leads and CNY 24,000.

## Budget decision

The small tests imply placement costs of CNY 300 per qualified search lead and CNY 200 per qualified industry-email lead. Applying those rates gives:

| Conditional plan | Setup | Search | Industry email | Total | Estimated qualified leads |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Current mixed allocation | 6,000 | 6,000 | 12,000 | 24,000 | 80 |
| **Proposed mixed allocation** | **6,000** | **9,000** | **18,000** | **33,000** | **120** |
| All-email alternative | 6,000 | 0 | 24,000 | 30,000 | 120 |
| All-email within ceiling | 6,000 | 0 | 18,000 | 24,000 | 90 |

Amounts are CNY. All lead figures extrapolate one small test per channel; prices, reach and performance at scale remain unverified.

The draft's 80 leads and CNY 300 all-in cost per lead are arithmetically consistent. Correct rescaling holds setup fixed: **6,000 + 1.5 × 18,000 = 33,000**, or CNY 275 all-in per lead. The draft's CNY 36,000 proposal is CNY 3,000 higher than this estimate.

Retaining the mix preserves search coverage but costs CNY 3,000 more than the all-email 120-lead scenario. All-email requires only CNY 6,000 above the ceiling, but sacrifices search coverage and concentrates delivery on one unproven-at-scale channel. Choose it only if the owner approves that trade-off and email capacity is confirmed.

If the ceiling remains fixed, even putting every placement yuan into the historically cheaper channel yields only 90 estimated leads. Reaching 120 would require an average placement cost of CNY 150 per qualified lead, versus observed costs of CNY 200–300. Do not assume that improvement. A lower target needs explicit approval.

## Executable schedule proposal

The marketer has 18 assigned hours against 16 available over two weeks; moving work between those weeks alone cannot fix this. Keep the writer at seven hours in each week and propose:

| Period | Marketer activities | Hours |
|---|---|---:|
| Week 1 | Setup review 3; partner coordination 2; initial tracking 1; placement buying 2 | 8 |
| Week 2 | Remaining buying 1; placement management 3; lead qualification 3; measurement 1 | 8 |
| Week 3 | Reporting | 2 |

This preserves every activity's total hours, splitting the two measurement hours between initial tracking and subsequent measurement. Complete setup review, tracking and partner coordination before the first paid placement. The consequence requiring approval is reporting in week 3 and a paid window beginning late in week 1, with remaining placements bought in week 2. The lead estimate is conditional on that shorter exposure being sufficient.

## Before commitment

The launch owner should approve the budget/channel choice and timing change. The marketer should obtain quotes, placement availability, delivery dates and capacity at the proposed spend; verify that tracking can be ready within the proposed split; and confirm that management and qualification hours cover expected lead arrivals. Confirm week-3 reporting availability. If these checks fail, revise timing, scope or the budget/target decision before ordering placements.

Count leads only when all three requirements are evidenced: an identified team need, valid work email and permission for follow-up. Release paid commitments only after approvals and prerequisite checks are complete.
