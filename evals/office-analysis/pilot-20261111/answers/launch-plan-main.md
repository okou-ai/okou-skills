# Launch recommendation

Proceed with preparation for an **email-only plan targeting 120 qualified leads**, conditional on approval of a **CNY 30,000 total budget** and the checks below. Hold setup and placement commitments until approval. This preserves the lead target at the lowest modeled cost using the supplied history, but requires CNY 6,000 above the ceiling and concentrates delivery risk in one channel.

## Budget decision

Historical placement cost per qualified lead was CNY 300 for search (3,000 ÷ 10) and CNY 200 for industry email (4,000 ÷ 20). The CNY 6,000 setup fee is fixed and must be added once.

**Planning estimates, assuming unchanged channel economics and sufficient capacity:**

| Plan | Search spend | Email spend | Total including setup | Estimated leads |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Draft allocation | 6,000 | 12,000 | 24,000 | 80 |
| Email-only within ceiling | 0 | 18,000 | 24,000 | 90 |
| Same mix, 120 leads | 9,000 | 18,000 | 33,000 | 120 |
| Recommended email-only proposal | 0 | 24,000 | 30,000 | 120 |

All amounts are CNY. The draft’s 80 leads and CNY 300 all-in cost per lead are arithmetically consistent. Its CNY 36,000 proposal scales the fixed setup fee incorrectly: 120 leads at the same mix require 1.5 × 18,000 in placements + 6,000 setup = **33,000**, not 36,000.

Approve the recommended CNY 6,000 increase (25%) and channel concentration together. Retaining both channels instead costs an estimated CNY 3,000 more. If the ceiling remains fixed, email-only supports an estimated 90 leads, requiring explicit approval of a 30-lead target reduction. Without either approval, hold launch. Meeting 120 within the ceiling would require CNY 150 placement cost per lead, 25% below the best observed rate; there is no evidence supporting that improvement.

## Proposed two-week execution

The draft gives the marketer 10 hours in week 1 and 8 in week 2, against 8 available each week: an overall two-hour deficit. Moving work alone cannot fix it.

**Proposed staffing change:** transfer the existing two reporting hours to the writer, split into one hour of report preparation in week 1 and one hour of final reporting in week 2. Confirm that the writer can perform this work and that the split is viable; retain every supplied activity-hour estimate.

| Owner | Week 1 | Week 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Marketer | Setup review 3h; partner coordination 2h; initial tracking/measurement 1h; placement buying 2h = **8h** | Remaining buying 1h; placement management 3h; lead checking 3h; measurement 1h = **8h** |
| Writer | Existing work 7h + report preparation 1h = **8h** | Existing work 7h + final reporting 1h = **8h** |

Complete setup review, initial tracking and partner coordination before buying and activating placements. Begin paid delivery only after these prerequisites, with remaining buying in week 2. This shortens the paid window and leaves no staffing buffer. If the reporting transfer cannot be confirmed, this schedule is not ready for commitment; revise scope or timing with owner approval.

## Checks before commitment

- Obtain current quotes and confirm email inventory, scalable lead volume and delivery dates within the shortened paid window. One small test per channel does not establish future performance.
- Verify the setup scope and quoted placement totals fit the approved budget; revise the forecast if prices or conversion economics differ.
- Confirm writer ownership, task dependencies and that these checks fit the supplied activity estimates; otherwise revise capacity before launch.
- Test tracking and qualification before activation. Count a lead only with an identified team need, valid work email and permission for follow-up. Preserve measurement and lead-checking time throughout.
