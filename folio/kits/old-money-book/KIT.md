# Old-money Classic Book

**Style:** Quiet editorial company book with ivory paper, espresso type, champagne seal, sage headings and flowing spread bands. Sharp serif display typography, sketch slots beside text, portrait placeholders, and an ivory-to-espresso back cover. Suitable design directions: company profiles, law, finance, real estate, schools and heritage brands.

**Page roles (8):** 1 cover—masthead, italic line, seal, large sketch slot; 2 contents with sketch slot; 3 studio—sage heading field, quote, statistic and text; 4–5 services spread—build/design/media, sketch across fold, flowing sage timeline band; 6 print work—sketch and champagne field; 7 people—sage heading, two rectangular portrait slots, promise-copy slot; 8 back—sign-off, sketch slot, espresso contact field with flowing edge.

**Palette:** Theme recommendations: sage `#7D8B6A`, champagne `#C9A96E`. CSS roles: paper `#F6F1E7`, ink `#2B1D16`, secondary ink `#5E4E43`, espresso field `#3B2A20`, tint `#EFE7DA`, accent `#C9A96E`, support `#7D8B6A`.

**Typography:** Bodoni Moda 400/italic 400/500 for editorial display; Familjen Grotesk 400/600 for labels. Source Serif 4 with generic serif fallback carries the body role formerly assigned to Times New Roman. The body role and sizes remain unchanged; actual line wrapping requires assembled-layout review.

**Imagery roles:** On-topic coloured-pencil sketch slots; flowing bands; rectangular portrait placeholders. Generic vectors are included, not photographs or actual sketches. Original `paper-ivory.svg` provides the paper overlay without an undocumented source bitmap or a texture-generation dependency.

## Assembly and use

- A4 portrait, 210 × 297 mm; recommended bleed: 3 mm. Preserve the listed page roles and cross-fold continuity.
- Run `kit.py new old-money-book <fresh-project> --name brochure`: it supplies `fonts/fonts.css`, licensed local Latin fonts and `folio.css`. Raw kit.html is a source template, not the assembled output.
- HTML text and image slots marked `data-kit-sample` are demonstration content, not verified claims. Replace sample company identity, contacts, project counts, prices, timelines and capability labels with verified project content before use.
- Replace neutral mark/photo/portrait slots only with appropriately sourced project assets; do not infer a real brand affiliation from sample technology names.
- Original photo-credit sentences were removed because those photographs are not in this bundle. Generic vector assets retain their source bytes and the upstream Apache-2.0 license accompanies this curated bundle.
- After every adaptation, render and inspect all pages; check contrast, font loading, overflow, bleed and preflight. Template validation does not certify final copy, print production or legal clearance.
