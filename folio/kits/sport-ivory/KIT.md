# Sport Layers Ivory

**Style:** Warm, layered editorial magazine for studios, agencies, consultancies and services. Espresso cover, large masthead behind a cut-out-person slot, sage panel and offset photo slot. Ivory reading pages use low-opacity shared image slots, champagne gutter-spanning note boxes, sage bands, large ivory numerals and sketch slots crossing the fold.

**Page roles (12):** 1 layered cover; 2–3 contents and studio with gutter box; 4–5 large word, layered photo slots, service lists with neutral marks, fold-crossing sketch and sage timeline band; 6–7 dark systems opener and light stack/industries page with gutter box; 8–9 design/media words, sage field, photo slot, fold-crossing sketch and print list; 10–11 statistic, generic stars, price, sage steps band and directors with circular portrait slots, gutter box; 12 contact back cover.

**Palette:** Theme recommendations: sage `#7D8B6A`, champagne `#C9A96E`. CSS roles: paper `#F6F1E7`, ink `#2B1D16`, secondary ink `#5E4E43`, espresso field `#3B2A20`, tint `#EFE7DA`, accent `#C9A96E`, support `#7D8B6A`.

**Typography:** Big Shoulders Display 800/900 for mastheads, big words and numerals; Bodoni Moda 400/italic 400/500 for editorial headings; Familjen Grotesk 400/600 for labels. Source Serif 4 with generic serif fallback replaces Times New Roman in the body role, retaining existing sizes and spacing. Review actual wrapping after assembly.

**Imagery roles:** Duotone/shaded background slots, one cut-out-person slot, layered rectangular photo slots, coloured-pencil sketch slots with palette `#3B2A20,#7D8B6A,#C9A96E,#F6F1E7`, circular portraits and neutral service marks. No stock photography or real technology-logo files are included. Original procedural SVG textures replace the undocumented paper/grain recipe-output PNGs; no runtime texture generator is required.
