# Back cover

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** A deliberate closing page that connects to the cover without repeating it.

**Composition**
- Echo one front-cover device: an arc, offset slab, crop or colour field. Mirror or rotate it only if that helps the closing layout.
- Reduce the cover's visual intensity: one short sign-off, then a compact contact block. A plain contact-only treatment is also valid for a restrained document.
- Keep logo, contact details and optional QR on a shared alignment; leave breathing room around the sign-off.

**Starting points**
- On A4, try a 24–40pt sign-off and contact text around 9–11pt; check at final size.
- Include only approved, relevant contact details. Verify every URL, phone number and address.
- For a QR, reserve roughly 20–25mm plus the required quiet zone; actual size depends on payload and print quality. Test the final printed size on a phone.

**Watch for:** A second front cover with equal visual noise; tiny low-contrast contacts; QR placed across a fold or over a textured image.

**Construction:** Reuse the cover's geometry and palette, not its exact arrangement. Keep contacts as readable text and the QR separate from background artwork.
