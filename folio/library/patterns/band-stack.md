# Band stack

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** Repeated catalog section openers that must remain recognisable while products change.

**Composition**
1. A wide photo band occupies roughly 40–55% of the page height, reaching the top and side edges if bleed is available.
2. A title sits at the lower photo edge or in a narrow solid band below it.
3. A quieter content zone holds two or three columns of features; an optional photo strip anchors the foot.

**Starting points**
- Choose one band height, title anchor and heading scale for a family of sections. Test the longest title before locking them.
- Crop images around a consistent focal zone; do not stretch different subjects to fill the band.
- If a translation is secondary, put it directly below the title with a clear size contrast. Equal-status languages need a balanced lockup instead.
- Keep text off busy photographic detail; a solid title band is preferable to an unreadable overlay.

**Watch for:** Arbitrary band-height changes, titles drifting between pages, or an overcrowded content zone competing with the opener image.

**Construction:** Establish explicit photo, title and content rows. Align content columns to title edges; use the same image-band dimensions across the section system.
