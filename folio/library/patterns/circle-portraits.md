# Circle portraits

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** A compact team or leadership grid with photographs from different settings.

**Composition**
- Repeat equal-diameter circular crops, with name and role directly beneath each portrait.
- Align eyes at a common relative height, roughly 40% down the crop, while retaining hair and chin comfortably.
- Use consistent head scale; equal circle sizes alone do not create consistency.

**Starting points**
- Try 22–32mm portrait diameters on A4, with readable 9–11pt names and slightly quieter roles.
- Keep row and column spacing regular; allow enough label height for the longest name and role.
- If a hierarchy is required, express it through grouping or position. Use connector lines only when they reflect a verified reporting relationship.
- Harmonise backgrounds gently, without altering identity or implying that unrelated people were photographed together.

**Watch for:** Mixed crop scales, eyes at random heights, cramped labels, or decorative connectors that invent an organisation chart.

**Construction:** Apply a circular clip to each image and adjust its focal position independently. Check all crops and labels at final page size.
