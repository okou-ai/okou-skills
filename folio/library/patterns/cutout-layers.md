# Cut-out layers

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** An energetic editorial cover where overlap, rather than a tiled grid, establishes depth.

**Composition: back to front**
1. A flat ground, optionally with a subdued context photo at roughly 12–25% opacity.
2. One solid colour field or a rectangular secondary image.
3. A masthead or large headline.
4. A cleanly isolated subject crossing the field or headline edge.
5. A small foreground detail, if it contributes information, then essential cover lines.

**Control the depth**
- Start with three or four layers; add another only when its edge clarifies the stack. Not every cover needs texture or extra imagery.
- Use deliberate overlaps and scale differences; shadows are optional, not a substitute for clear silhouettes.
- Keep faces, eyes and essential lettering unobstructed. Anchor the subject to a panel, page edge or plausible crop rather than letting it float.
- Use supplied, owned or appropriately licensed images. Illustrative stock must not be presented as an actual team member or event participant.

**Watch for:** Edge halos, competing focal subjects, unreadable mastheads, or unrelated images added merely to fill space.

**Construction:** Set an explicit stacking order. Inspect transparent edges against the actual ground; check both screen rendering and final output rather than assuming export preserves effects.
