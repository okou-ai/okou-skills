# Cut-out masthead cover

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** A magazine-style cover led by one identifiable subject and a strong publication name.

**Composition**
- Stack ground → masthead → isolated figure → cover lines.
- Let the subject overlap a limited portion of the masthead; the name must remain recognisable, especially for an unfamiliar publication.
- Put three to five short cover lines on the subject's open side. Establish one main story and quieter supporting stories.

**Starting points**
- On A4, a heavy masthead around 90–150pt can establish scale; fit to the actual name rather than forcing a fixed size.
- Reserve accent colour for a few significant words or numbers. Choose light or dark remaining text according to the actual background contrast.
- Keep type off the face and eyes; move or recrop the subject if the free side is too narrow.
- Add at most one badge if it conveys real information. Include issue/date/barcode only when supplied and relevant.

**Watch for:** A haloed silhouette, too much masthead occlusion, every story at equal weight, or tiny cover lines scattered around the figure.

**Construction:** Use an image with a clean transparent edge or a carefully prepared mask. Check the masthead at thumbnail size and the subject edge at full output size.
