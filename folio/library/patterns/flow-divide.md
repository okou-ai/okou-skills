# Flow divide

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** An occasional flowing colour field that links two facing pages or softens a rigid layout.

**Composition**
- Treat the field as one continuous shape across the spread, then split it at the fold for individual pages.
- Start with one broad curve, or at most two crests. A wave amplitude around 4–8% of the band height gives movement without dominating the content.
- Keep photos rectangular when the curved field already supplies enough shape contrast; a straight divider remains valid for more structured material.

**Starting points**
- Leave roughly 6mm between text and the highest point of the curved boundary, then adjust for trim and type scale.
- Keep important content farther from the spine according to binding requirements; the field may cross the fold, but text should not.
- Use one palette colour for the field. Repeated multicolour waves quickly become the main subject.

**Watch for:** Rippling edges, curves passing through text, or using the device on every page regardless of the document's rhythm.

**Construction:** Draw a simple SVG path in spread coordinates and clip each half consistently. Check that the halves meet; allow for physical binding misalignment rather than promising a seamless printed join.
