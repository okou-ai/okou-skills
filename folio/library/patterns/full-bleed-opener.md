# Full-bleed opener

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** A clear chapter change led by one strong photograph rather than boxes or rules.

**Composition**
- Let a photograph fill one page, with narrative on the facing page, or extend the image across the spread when its crop supports that choice.
- Place a short headline in genuine negative space: sky, wall, water or another low-detail area.
- Keep the text page quieter, with a clear heading, short deck and readable columns.

**Starting points**
- Select the photograph for both subject and title space. Never solve a poor crop by putting type over faces.
- Use one strong weight contrast; an accent word is optional, not a requirement.
- If contrast needs help, try a local scrim behind the title before darkening the entire image. Check that the photograph still carries the story.
- Keep faces and critical detail about 15mm or more from the spine as a starting allowance; confirm the actual binding requirements.
- A page number may be omitted from the image page if navigation remains clear elsewhere.

**Watch for:** A boxed image that weakens the intended chapter break, unreadable title contrast, or a spread crop that loses its subject in the fold.

**Construction:** Extend images to the required bleed outside trim, retain sufficient resolution, and inspect both single-page and facing-page views.
