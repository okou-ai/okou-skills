# Gutter box

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** Linking a short note on one page to supporting evidence on the facing page.

**Composition**
- Run one flat colour rectangle across the spread, with matching top and bottom edges at the spine.
- Left half: a small label and one sentence. Right half: a supporting image, quote or one to two related facts.
- Keep text and images page-local; only the ground needs to span the fold.

**Starting points**
- Use one support or accent colour and enough internal padding for a clear note hierarchy.
- Begin with at least 9–15mm clearance from the spine for content, increasing it for tight bindings. Ask the printer for the actual safe area.
- A centre spread in a saddle-stitched booklet often aligns more naturally than separate leaves, but still needs a proof check.
- Prefer a flat ground over thin cross-fold rules or precise image joins that expose registration differences.

**Watch for:** Sentences broken by the gutter, unrelated facts sharing a box, repeated boxes flattening every spread, or assuming perfect physical alignment.

**Construction:** Define the rectangle in spread coordinates and clip the two halves at the fold. Verify matching geometry in facing-page view and readable padding in single-page view.
