# Map reach

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** Communicating verified offices, service locations or geographic coverage.

**Composition**
- Use a quiet single-colour base map, a small set of pins and directly associated labels.
- Thin leader lines can move labels out of crowded regions; try 0.5pt at print size and inspect legibility.
- Pair the map with a concise location list or summary, rather than adding unrelated charts merely to fill space.

**Data and geography checks**
- Distinguish offices from service areas and customer locations; use separate symbols or explicit labels if showing more than one.
- Plot only verified locations. A pin does not establish nationwide coverage; a shaded region needs a defensible coverage definition.
- Choose an accurate geographic source and projection suitable for the region. Check map licensing, required credits and applicable boundary requirements.
- Prefer an inset or numbered key to overlapping labels. Add a text equivalent for readers who cannot interpret the map.

**Watch for:** Decorative globes, invented pins, unreadable small regions, or an outline that misrepresents jurisdictional boundaries.

**Construction:** Use a licensed vector base when available. Align pins to actual coordinates before simplifying; verify labels and boundaries after export.
