# Mosaic gallery

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** Combining several related event, product or place photographs into one coherent visual unit.

**Composition**
- Begin with a regular grid and narrow 1–2mm gutters on A4.
- Make one strong image a two-by-two tile; let the remaining images occupy simple grid units.
- Keep captions in a nearby aligned column or keyed list when individual captions below every tile would fragment the whole.

**Starting points**
- Choose one consistent colour treatment without obscuring meaningful product or scene differences.
- Align the grid to page columns. An optional bleed on one outer edge can give the mosaic more presence.
- Place lower-resolution images in smaller tiles only if their effective resolution is still sufficient; resizing cannot rescue unusable material.
- Crop each image for its own subject, while keeping people and important detail away from tile edges.

**Watch for:** Every tile having a unique size, accidental slivers between rows, repeated frames and shadows, or captions too far from their images to identify them.

**Construction:** Use a grid with explicit spans and a consistent gap. Check each crop at final size and confirm that every photograph has a clear purpose.
