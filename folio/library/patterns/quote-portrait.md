# Quote + portrait

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** A signed editorial or leadership message where a person and a clear point of view matter.

**Composition**
- Combine a readable letter, one enlarged pull quote, a portrait and an attribution block.
- On a spread, place the portrait toward the outer edge rather than the spine. A gaze toward the text can guide attention when the supplied photo permits it.
- Use a circle, arc or rectangular crop that fits the document's existing geometry; do not introduce a new mask vocabulary just for this page.

**Starting points**
- On A4, try a 28–40pt heading and 18–24pt quote. Keep the letter at the document's normal body size.
- Two columns can suit a long message; one column is often better for a short one. Check line length rather than forcing a column count.
- Keep name and role readable, around 9–11pt at print size. A typed attribution is sufficient; use a signature image only if supplied and authorised.
- Quote exact approved wording and identify the speaker. Do not fabricate a testimonial or signature.

**Watch for:** A tiny headshot with no compositional role, an oversized quote overwhelming the letter, or a crop that loses the face at trim.

**Construction:** Keep body text, pull quote and attribution separate. Check that the portrait does not create narrow text slivers or conceal the reading order.
