# Sketch spot

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** A small explanatory or atmospheric illustration beside the text it supports.

**Composition**
- Let an open, irregular silhouette sit directly on the page ground rather than enclosing it in a photographic rectangle.
- Use a dark drawing colour with one or two support colours from the document palette; exposed paper creates lightness.
- Start around a quarter of the page area, then size according to the detail the illustration must communicate.

**Starting points**
- Use an original drawing, supplied artwork or an appropriately licensed asset. Do not trace or imitate a specific external artwork merely to obtain the effect.
- Place the spot near its related paragraph with a clear gap from text. Vary its scale only when the hierarchy benefits.
- A light ground usually preserves fine marks best; on dark grounds, test lighter strokes and transparent edges carefully.
- Keep essential labels as readable text. For raster linework, roughly 300 ppi at placed size is a starting target; fine detail may need more.

**Watch for:** Decorative drawings unrelated to the copy, every image treated identically, muddy transparent edges, or fine lines disappearing in print.

**Construction:** Use a supplied vector or transparent raster image. Check the final-size silhouette and line quality; this card does not assume an image-conversion tool is available.
