# Split panel

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** Separating a strong title zone from a photograph or a substantial text zone.

**Composition**
- Allocate roughly one-third or one-half of the page to a solid panel; use the remainder for image or narrative.
- Anchor the panel boundary to a grid line. Other proportions are valid when the title length and image subject justify them.
- Put the title and short deck on the panel with a clear shared alignment; keep the opposing zone quieter.

**Starting points**
- If the panel reaches trim, extend it to the required bleed rather than stopping at the page edge.
- Choose light or dark title text for sufficient contrast with the actual panel colour; do not assume white always works.
- For a crossing-photo variant, try a clear overlap of roughly 12mm on A4, large enough not to look accidental.
- A facing-page variation can use a panel on the left and narrative on the right; account for reading direction and binding.

**Watch for:** Tentative overlaps, a panel too narrow for the approved title, or every page repeating the same loud split with no pacing contrast.

**Construction:** Define panel and content columns first, then crop the image. Check title wrapping and image focus together before finalising the split.
