# Stat spread

> **Modified by Okou.** Adapted from Folio; [source and authorship](../INDEX.md#source-and-authorship).

**Use for:** Giving three to five verified metrics a clear visual hierarchy.

**Composition**
- Lead with one significant number and arrange supporting figures around a shared alignment, rather than making every metric an identical card.
- On A4, 40–90pt numerals can carry the page; keep labels and explanatory notes comfortably readable, around 9–11pt.
- Use one accent for the hero figure and quieter colours for supporting metrics.

**Data checks**
- Every value needs a unit, definition, time period and source. Include the denominator for percentages when it affects interpretation.
- Do not use proportional circles for unrelated measures such as revenue, headcount and satisfaction. Use unscaled numerals instead.
- For comparable non-negative values, circle area—not diameter—must encode magnitude: `r = Rmax × sqrt(value / max_value)`.
- Keep units and rounding consistent. Use lining figures for large numerals; tabular figures help where numbers must align.

**Watch for:** Decorative circles mistaken for quantitative encoding, numbers without dates, excessive rounding, or a dramatic scale that exaggerates a small difference.

**Construction:** Keep source notes near the display. Recompute chart geometry from verified data and inspect the final labels rather than relying on a visual resemblance to a chart.
