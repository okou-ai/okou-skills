# Manuscript requirements versus visual presets

Use supplied instructions/templates before selecting an authoring route. A named
standard, a generic visual mood and substantive domain expertise are different.
Do not claim that choosing a preset implements publication requirements.

## Named manuscript

For strict APA, IEEE or a journal/institution template, inspect the actual rules
and choose a route capable of implementing them. `--type academic` does **not**
implement APA/IEEE. The default cover and business-body scale must not override
a manuscript's typography, spacing, pagination or no-cover requirement.

When suitable, author through [DOCX](../../../docx/SKILL.md) and export to PDF;
check that route's actual fonts/layout and the rendered output too. If an
available tool cannot meet a constraint, disclose it and agree an alternative.
This is authoring selection, not a new PDF engine mode or compliance certificate.

## Visual-only document

For a generic, nonstandard academic-looking PDF or business/technical report,
choose an existing `--type` visual preset and content features independently.
[Presentation](../common/presentation.md) owns preset names, cover patterns,
tokens and override flags. Agent reasoning chooses the preset; `palette.py`
does not infer the document's domain or validate its facts, citations or analysis.

## Companion to Word

If Word and PDF are requested together, render the authored DOCX through its
route; do not independently redesign the PDF with this cover/body pipeline.
Deliver only the requested formats, even if a DOCX was used internally.
