# Write for the document's reader

Use when creating or substantially rewriting reader-facing prose in an Office
file. Follow the user's audience, genre, language, supplied structure and house
style. A wording-only edit stays within the requested scope.

## Turn working questions into informative titles

For formal reports, management memos, plans and research syntheses, use a title
that names the subject and purpose. Section headings should identify the specific
topic or state a supported finding, so the reader can understand the document's
outline without seeing the author's working notes.

An internal question can guide the analysis without becoming its heading. Replace
vague prompts, conversational coaching, slogans and colorful metaphors with the
subject, comparison, requirement or finding the section actually develops.
Prefer familiar, precise language; replacing a question with an abstract label
such as "strategic empowerment" does not make it more informative.

| Working or conversational heading | Reader-facing alternative for that section |
| --- | --- |
| 历史结果能支持什么 | 历史渠道表现与预测依据 |
| 150条需要哪些改变 | 150条合格线索目标的预算与人员要求 |
| 钱与线索如何对齐 | 分周预算与线索测算 |
| 分层做对照，而非一次“大混战” | 分层对照实验设计 |
| What do the numbers tell us? | Regional contributions to revenue growth |

These illustrate the transformation, not required section names. A finding-led
heading must keep the evidence's scope and uncertainty: a lower historical cost
does not establish the best future channel or guaranteed returns. Use a topic
heading when the evidence does not support a concise conclusion. Keep headings
at the same level comparable in scope and phrasing, and check each against its
actual section; do not add content merely to fit a heading.

Questions remain appropriate for FAQs, interviews, teaching materials, explicitly
framed research questions or a requested conversational style. Preserve required
template headings and user-supplied wording unless changing them is authorized.
Do not ban question marks, punctuation or plain language across all documents.

## Review the saved wording as well as its appearance

Read the document title and section headings together as an outline before
formatting, then check them again in the saved output. Remove headings that
depend on chat context, address the author rather than the reader, promise more
than the section establishes or expose internal review instructions. Keep
necessary assumptions and qualifications beside the claims they limit; avoid
repeating methodological cautions as a succession of headings.

Use direct, connected prose at the reader's level of technical detail. Explain
the finding, its basis and its consequence without narrating the agent's workflow.
A typography, schema or rendering pass does not perform this editorial review.
