# Qualification Guide

Qualification determines **whether the user can consider this person and what the user can do with the candidate record**. Start by identifying the decision the list must support. Use it to decide which facts could change inclusion, priority, or follow-up.

- **Fit asks whether the person meets the task's requirements and where they match.** Assess relevant experience, individual responsibilities, work, and practical conditions.
- **Intent asks whether the other party is willing to act now or is already taking relevant steps.** Use statements and actions that point to the requested next step. For example, a credit on a recent similar project can establish relevant experience. A current call for similar collaborations can establish willingness to consider that kind of work.

## Verify the match to the task (Fit)

Eligibility is an outcome of the Fit check and follows the original hard requirements. When the user needs to choose among eligible people, use the task's goal and preferences to compare how well they match.

Turn each hard requirement into a verifiable fact about a specific person in the relevant period or scope. Define what evidence is sufficient. Match what the task calls for to the candidate's documented responsibilities, actions, or results. Start with the requirement most likely to determine eligibility and verify the person's identity, role, and contribution at the relevant time.

Record the supporting fact, original source, and location for each key judgment. Distinguish event, publication, and verification dates. Use [Tools and evidence](tool-evidence.md) to judge what the material supports.

- **Pass.** Evidence supports the hard requirements about Fit. End this part of verification. Count the person as eligible once all hard requirements pass.
- **Ineligible.** A specific fact contradicts a hard requirement. Record it and end any Intent or profile research for recommending this person.
- **Unverified.** Key evidence is missing, or attribution or timing is unresolved. Look for a source that can close the gap directly. If none is available, keep the person as a lead with the facts established and the requirement still open, then move to other candidates.

### When to compare Fit

For identity verification, an eligibility check, or a list of qualified people, provide the verification result and evidence. **Report a Fit percentage when differences in suitability could change the user's choice.** Derive the criteria from the user's goal and preferences. Specify what evidence shows a strong or partial match and what gap could change the decision. Define what combination warrants 100% and what lower bands mean. Apply any user-specified weights and the same rubric across batches. Count each fact once.

Finish the comparison when the evidence explains the ranking. Report a single whole-number percentage from 0% to 100% with the key facts, gaps, and sources. Prioritize gaps that could change the ranking. If a gap remains unresolved, score the verified matches provisionally and identify what could change the result. Recheck affected candidates if the task changes.

## Check intent relevant to the next step (Intent)

Check Intent once the hard requirements about Fit and the key matches needed for the next step have evidence, and recent willingness or action could change what the user does next. Investigate gaps that only affect ranking alongside Intent. If the user makes an Intent signal a hard requirement, verify the other requirements first, then use the signal to complete the eligibility decision.

Define who would take which action and which signals count within the task's time window. Record the actor, date, and source for each signal. Explain how it affects whether, when, or how to follow up. Attribute organizational developments to the organization and individual actions to the person. If one source supports both Fit and Intent, identify the fact it supports for each judgment.

| Level or status | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **High** | An explicit, current expression of willingness, or direct action showing that the party is pursuing this opportunity or a close match. |
| **Medium** | Recent activity or a specific indirect signal suggests that the party may be willing to pursue this kind of opportunity. Explain how it relates to the next step. Willingness still needs confirmation. |
| **Low** | A clear contrary signal, or only identifiable weak signals after reviewing relevant sources. State the evidence and the scope checked. |
| **Unverified** | Available evidence is insufficient to assign a level. State whether the check has yet to be made, relevant signals were unavailable, access was blocked, or evidence conflicted. |
| **Not applicable** | The user's decision does not depend on the other party's willingness or timing. |

End the Intent check once the evidence supports a level and the next step. If the remaining sources cannot answer the key question or are likely to repeat a known signal, record the supported level or mark it Unverified. State what to confirm with the person.

## When to enrich candidate profiles

Summarize the verified facts in a record the user can act on. Explain who the person is, what they did, and what evidence supports their match. When follow-up matters, include relevant signals, available contact channels, and what to verify next. When the user wants the strongest match, rank by Fit and use Intent to prioritize follow-up among candidates with similar Fit.

Retain user-requested fields and add details that help comparison or follow-up. If the task depends on reach through a creator's account, record follower or subscriber counts, recent performance of relevant work, and the verification date by platform. Record the creator's location, intended market, actual audience geography, and delivery conditions separately. Use audience analytics with a defined scope and date for actual audience geography. Include contact channels and open questions where useful.

Reuse evidence already collected. Finish the profile when it explains eligibility, any relevant comparison, and the next step. Investigate further when a fact could change the decision and a source could answer it directly. Update the record when new evidence changes the judgment. Record details that require the person's input as specific follow-up questions.
