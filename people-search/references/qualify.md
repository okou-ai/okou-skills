# Qualification Guide

State what the user will do with the list. Verify whether each person meets the requirements, then compare candidates or assess timing when the decision calls for it. Make the conclusion, evidence, and open questions clear in the delivered record.

## Verify task fit first (Fit)

Translate each hard requirement into a fact you can verify. Specify the person, time period or scope, and evidence sufficient to pass. Start with the requirement most likely to block inclusion. Match the capabilities, responsibilities, outputs, or working conditions the task calls for to the person's own work and documented circumstances. For key judgments, record the fact, original link, and passage or timestamp. Distinguish event, publication, and verification dates. Use [Tools and evidence](tool-evidence.md) to judge what the material supports.

- **Pass.** Include the candidate once evidence supports every hard requirement. If intent is a hard requirement, verify the other conditions first and complete the eligibility decision during the Intent check.
- **Ineligible.** Evidence shows that a hard requirement is unmet. Record the reason and move to other candidates.
- **Unverified.** Key evidence is missing, or identity, timing, or attribution is disputed. Look for a source that directly addresses the gap. If none is available, keep the person as a lead, state what is known and missing, and move to other candidates.

**Use Fit percentages to compare candidates.** Score candidates when the user needs to choose among eligible people, rank them, or prioritize by suitability. Use the task's goal and preferences to define the criteria, evidence standards, and what full and lower scores mean. Apply any user-specified weights and the same rubric across batches. Count each fact once. Give one whole-number percentage from 0% to 100% with its key evidence. When the request is limited to verifying identities or eligibility, or listing everyone who qualifies, provide the verification result and evidence.

End eligibility verification once the hard requirements are resolved. When comparing candidates, gather enough evidence to explain the differences that affect ranking. If a material ranking gap cannot be resolved, give a provisional score based on verified matches and record the gap. Recheck affected candidates when the task changes.

## Check intent when it affects the next step (Intent)

Check Intent when evidence supports the key match and the result could change whether, when, or how to follow up. Resolve Fit gaps that could change whether the user would consider the person first. Gaps that only affect relative ranking can be checked alongside Intent.

Specify who would take which action and which signal dates count. Look for public statements or actions that show current willingness. Record the actor, date, source, and effect on the next step. Attribute a company's actions to the company and a person's actions to that person. A credit on a similar project establishes experience. A current call for similar collaborations can establish willingness to consider that work. When one source provides both kinds of evidence, state what each fact supports.

| Level or status | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **High** | Explicit, current willingness or direct action advancing this opportunity or a close match. |
| **Medium** | Recent activity or a specific indirect signal suggests possible willingness. Explain how it relates to the next step. Willingness still needs confirmation. |
| **Low** | A clear contrary signal, or identifiable weak signals after reviewing the relevant sources. |
| **Unverified** | Evidence is insufficient to assign a level. State whether the check is pending, no signal was found, access was blocked, or sources conflict. |
| **Not applicable** | The user's decision does not depend on the other party's willingness or timing. |

End the check when the evidence supports a level and the next step. If remaining sources are likely to repeat known signals or cannot answer the key question, record the supported assessment and open questions.

## When to enrich candidate profiles

Turn Fit and applicable Intent evidence into a candidate record the user can act on. Explain who the person is, what they did themselves, which evidence connects them to this task, and what to confirm next. When the user wants the strongest matches, rank by Fit and use Intent to prioritize follow-up among candidates with similar Fit.

Retain requested fields. Add facts that could affect selection or follow-up, along with any relevant public contact channels and their ownership. If the task depends on reach through a creator's account, record follower or subscriber counts, recent performance of relevant work, and verification dates by platform. Track location, intended market, actual audience geography, and delivery conditions separately. Use dated audience analytics with a defined scope to verify geographic shares.

Start with evidence already collected. Before another lookup, identify the missing fact, the decision it could change, and a source that could answer it directly. Update eligibility, Fit, or Intent if new evidence changes the judgment. Deliver the profile once it supports the user's decision. Turn facts that require the person's input into specific follow-up questions.
