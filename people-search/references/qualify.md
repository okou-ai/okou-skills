# Qualification Guide

Qualification determines **whether the user can consider this person and what the user can do with the candidate record**. Start by identifying the decision the list must support. Use it to decide which facts could change inclusion, priority, or follow-up.

## Establish who qualifies

Turn each hard requirement into a verifiable fact about a specific person in the relevant period or scope. Define what evidence is sufficient. Match what the task calls for to the candidate's documented responsibilities, actions, or results. Start with the requirement most likely to determine eligibility and verify the person's identity, role, and contribution at the relevant time.

Record the supporting fact, original source, and location for each key judgment. Distinguish event, publication, and verification dates. Use [Tools and evidence](tool-evidence.md) to judge what the material supports.

- **Pass.** Evidence supports every hard requirement. Count the person as eligible.
- **Ineligible.** A specific fact contradicts a hard requirement. Record it.
- **Unverified.** Key evidence is missing, or attribution or timing is unresolved. Keep the person as a lead with the facts established and the requirement still open.

## Use Fit when the user needs to choose among candidates

Score eligible candidates when differences in suitability could change selection, ranking, or who the user considers first. For identity verification, an eligibility check, or a list of qualified people, provide the verification result and evidence.

Derive comparison criteria from the user's decision and preferences. For each criterion, specify the fact to verify, what evidence shows a strong or partial match, and what gap could change the decision. Before scoring, define what combination warrants 100% and what lower bands mean. Apply any user-specified weights and the same rubric across batches. Count each fact once.

Report a single whole-number percentage from 0% to 100%, with the facts, gaps, and sources that explain the ranking. If evidence needed for comparison is incomplete, score the verified matches provisionally and identify what could change the ranking. Recheck affected candidates if the task changes.

## Check Intent when it can change the next step

Check Intent once the evidence is sufficient for the user to consider the person and a signal of willingness or recent action could change what to do next. Resolve gaps that could change eligibility or whether the person is worth considering first. Investigate gaps that only affect ranking alongside Intent. If the user makes an Intent signal a hard requirement, verify the other requirements first, then use the signal to complete the eligibility decision.

Define who would take which action and which signals count within the task's time window. Check for public statements or actions relevant to that step. Record who acted, when, and the source. Attribute organizational developments to the organization and individual actions to the person. Explain how the signal affects whether, when, or how to follow up.

| Level or status | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **High** | An explicit, current expression of willingness, or direct action showing that the party is pursuing this opportunity or a close match. |
| **Medium** | Recent relevant activity or a specific indirect signal that explains why follow-up is timely. Willingness still needs confirmation. |
| **Low** | A clear contrary signal, or only identifiable weak signals after reviewing relevant sources. State the evidence and the scope checked. |
| **Unverified** | Available evidence is insufficient to assign a level. State whether the check has yet to be made, relevant signals were unavailable, access was blocked, or evidence conflicted. |
| **Not applicable** | The user's decision does not depend on the other party's willingness or timing. |

## When to enrich candidate profiles

Summarize the verified facts in a record the user can act on. Explain who the person is, what they did, and what evidence supports their match. When follow-up matters, include relevant signals, available contact channels, and what to verify next. When the user wants the strongest match, rank by Fit and use Intent to prioritize follow-up among candidates with similar Fit.

Retain user-requested fields and add details that help comparison or follow-up. If the task depends on reach through a creator's account, record follower or subscriber counts, recent performance of relevant work, and the verification date by platform. Record the creator's location, intended market, actual audience geography, and delivery conditions separately. Use audience analytics with a defined scope and date for actual audience geography. Include contact channels and open questions where useful.

Reuse evidence already collected. Investigate further when a fact could change the decision and a source could answer it directly. Update the record when new evidence changes the judgment. State what to confirm next for unresolved gaps.
