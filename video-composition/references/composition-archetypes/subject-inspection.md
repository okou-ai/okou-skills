# Subject Inspection

One object, body region, place, or record receives only the annotations needed to understand it.

## Frame sketch

~~~text
+----------------------------------------------------------+
| PRIMARY SUBJECT        TWO MEANINGFUL CALLOUTS           |
|                                                          |
|        ONE DOMINANT FIELD · PURPOSEFUL NEGATIVE SPACE    |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
~~~

The sketch describes a spatial relationship, not coordinates. Mirror, stretch, crop, simplify, fuse, or replace it when real content needs another arrangement.

## Allowed variations

- Change orientation, asymmetry, presenter side, presenter representation (`full` or grounded `head-shoulders`), density, and the number of supporting elements.
- Add or remove the presenter; reclaim the territory completely when removed.
- Combine this relationship with another archetype or invent a new composition.
- Use any one named palette variant without treating it as a different template.

## Layout rules

- Preserve one dominant reading priority and one coherent background field.
- Keep critical text at video-readable sizes; add or reorganize scenes before shrinking.
- Keep face, hands, leaders, labels, data, media, and essential shapes disjoint.
- Use the brief rail, deadline block, decision field, and rollout marker only when they carry meaning.
- Do not add page numbers, slide counters, folios, template labels, or pagination UI.
